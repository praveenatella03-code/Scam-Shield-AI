ScamShield AI

Detect Fake Jobs & Online Scams. Protect Yourself.

Fake job offers and online scams (phishing, OTP theft, lottery, loan apps, digital arrest, UPI refunds) all use the same tricks. ScamShield AI is one project with two detectors: a Fake Job Detector and a Scam Message Detector, each with its own ML model and rules.

Advanced Features

🧠 Live ML model

Trained in your browser on a 300-post dataset, with precision, recall, F1 and a confusion matrix.

🖍️ Phrase highlighting

Suspicious wording is marked directly in the text.

🏷️ Scam-type detection

Advance-fee, data theft, task, MLM or cheque scam.

🌐 Domain & link analysis

Free email, odd TLDs, hyphen-heavy, IP and shortened links.

📊 Dashboard

Stats, score trend, CSV export, history management.

⚖️ Compare & batch scan

✅ Verify & report

💬 Scam message detector

🧩 Smart Scan
